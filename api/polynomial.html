Polynomial surface

Polynomial surface by least squares with the adequacy checks of Zelensky & Donchenko (2025).

class pitmap.core.polynomial.PolynomialSurface(degree=2)[source]

Bivariate polynomial surface z = f(x, y) fitted by least squares.

The surface is a sum of monomials x**i * y**j with i + j <= degree. Coordinates are centred and scaled internally so high degrees stay numerically stable; coef_ is in those scaled units.

After fit the three adequacy indicators of Zelensky & Donchenko (2025) are available:

r2_

Coefficient of determination. The paper reads 0.5 to 0.8 as satisfactory, above 0.8 as highly reliable, below 0.5 as ineffective.

variance_ratio_

(var(z) - var(fitted)) / var(z) * 100, the percentage by which the surface’s variance departs from the data’s. The paper accepts differences under 20 percent; a polynomial that interpolates every point can have R2 = 1 and still fail this check by oscillating between points.

lag1_autocorrelation_

r_1 of the input z in the order given. This describes the data, not the fit; the paper treats r_1 > 0.7 as a trend worth modelling.

Parameters:

degree (int) – Polynomial degree, 0 to 5 in the original system. Higher degrees are allowed but rarely adequate.
