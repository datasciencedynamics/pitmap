Polygonal (nearest region)

The oldest method in mine surveying. Every location takes the value of its closest survey point, so the surface is piecewise constant over the Voronoi cell (polygon of influence) of each point:

\[z(\mathbf{x}) = z_{k^*}, \qquad k^* = \arg\min_k \lVert \mathbf{x} - \mathbf{p}_k \rVert\]

In the automated system described by Zelensky and Lysenko (2023), polygons are built around boreholes inside a block, clipped to the block contour, and the area of each clipped polygon weights that borehole’s thickness and grade in the block’s reserve estimate.

from pitmap import NearestRegion
model = NearestRegion().fit(xy, z)
z_hat = model.predict(grid_xy)

The surface is exact at the survey points and discontinuous between them, which is fine for reserve accounting and poor for drawing.
